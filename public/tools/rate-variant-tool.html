No generated rate will be allowed to go below the value in a column you pick (e.g. your cost price).
Highlight the best-value variant
Marks whichever variant column has the lowest Grand Total — useful for picking the most competitive quote. Needs a Quantity column.
Export each variant as its own sheet
Adds extra tabs (Rate 1, Rate 2, ...) to the downloaded file, each with just that variant's columns — handy for separate bid documents.
